**howdy 👋**

nu11ware is a small workshop for open source Swift development.

What goes on here:

🔧 fixing bugs in the tools we use every day

🔒 poking at privacy apps to help keep them honest

🛠 building a few open-source things of our own

Join us!

## 💬 The Discord server

<img src="assets/server-icon.png" alt="The nu11ware server icon: a pair of white braces on a purple background." width="72" height="72" align="right">

nu11ware is a Discord server for people who write Swift and SwiftUI. You can talk about the language and Apple's frameworks, show what you are building, try the daily puzzle and follow the releases of the nu11ware projects, whether you started last week or several years ago.

[<img src="assets/join-discord.svg" alt="Join Discord" width="176" height="48">](https://discord.gg/d8KgvpAKcv)

## 🧭 Where to start

1. Read `#📜-rules`. You agree to them when you join.
2. Say who you are in `#🙋-introductions`.
3. Choose your roles in Channels & Roles, at the top of the channel list.
4. If you are new to Swift, read "Learning Swift" in `#📚-guides`.

## 📦 The nu11ware projects

nu11ware publishes these Swift projects on GitHub under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. Each release is announced in `#📣-announcements`.

- [swift-tunnel](https://github.com/nu11waredev/swift-tunnel): An implementation of the WireGuard protocol.
- [swift-vpn](https://github.com/nu11waredev/swift-vpn): A WireGuard VPN client for iOS and macOS.

## 🧩 The daily puzzle

Every day, `#🧩-puzzles` gets a short Swift program, and the question is what it prints. Reply with your answer inside a spoiler, written as `||your answer||`, so that other people can work it out first. The answer and the reason are posted in the same thread the next day.

> [!TIP]
> To be notified when a puzzle is posted, choose Puzzles in Channels & Roles.

## 📜 The rules in brief

The full rules are in `#📜-rules`. Treat other members with respect, keep each conversation to its channel's topic, do not advertise or send direct messages that nobody asked for, and never post passwords, keys, tokens or personal information. To report a message, choose Report Message from its menu, or send a direct message to the nu11ware account.
